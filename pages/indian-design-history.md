            <h1 class="idh__title" data-split>Influences on Indian Design History</h1>
            <p data-split data-delay="120">Professor Sudhakar Nadkarni's six-decade career coincided with, and in many ways helped define, the formation of modern design practice and education in India. At a moment when the discipline was still negotiating its identity in a post-independence context, his institutional work, teaching, and projects offered both a direction and a standard.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="idh__right">
            <p data-split data-delay="120">He founded institutions, shaped curricula, and trained designers who now occupy central positions across the field. His influence accumulated through the rigour he brought to seemingly ordinary problems, through the questions he asked before reaching for solutions, and through a refusal to treat design as ornament.</p>
            <p data-split data-delay="120">For those who studied under him or worked alongside him, Nadkarni represents something increasingly rare: a designer for whom practice and reflection were inseparable. His legacy in Indian design history lies not in any single object or institution, but in the habit of mind he modelled and passed on.</p>
