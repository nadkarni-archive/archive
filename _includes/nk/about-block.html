Prof. Sudhakar Nadkarni has spent a lifetime shaping how India thinks about design as a way of understanding and improving everyday life. His journey mirrors the evolution of modern Indian design itself.

He founded pioneering institutions, mentored generations, and quietly transformed how products, systems, and public spaces are imagined. His work bridges the local and the universal.

“Sudhakar is indeed very very special without a doubt; one of the most versatile designers India has produced in the many decades since independence. It is an honour to be his collaborator and his friend.”

Charles Correa
